How an account moves from audit to scale.

NGL just relaunched as a focused, one-operator practice — here's the process every account goes through. Case studies from live accounts will be added here as they're published.

The process

Four stages, same order, every time

Week 1

Audit

Tracking, pixel, past campaign data, and funnel reviewed. You get a written list of what's broken and what's wasted spend.

Week 2

Foundation

Tracking fixed, campaign structure rebuilt or set up, landing pages briefed against the offer.

Weeks 3–6

Test

First creative and audience tests go live. Budget stays controlled until we know what's actually working.

Ongoing

Scale

Budget shifts toward proven winners, new tests keep feeding the pipeline, reporting stays weekly and specific.
