How Kenyan Fintechs Can Lower Customer Acquisition Cost on Paid Ads

When a fintech founder tells me CAC is "too high," the platform is rarely the actual problem. In Kenya's fintech space specifically — lending apps, savings products, payments tools — CAC creep usually comes from three places, and only one of them has anything to do with Meta or Google's algorithm.

1. The offer is being sold before it's understood

Financial products need a beat of trust before a download or sign-up feels safe, especially with how much noise exists around predatory lending apps in Kenya right now. If your ad jumps straight to "download now" without addressing the skepticism a Kenyan user reasonably has about a new financial app, you're paying for clicks that bounce at the sign-up screen. Ads that briefly address legitimacy — licensing, data protection, who's behind the product — before the call to action tend to convert further down the funnel, even if the click-through rate looks slightly lower.

2. The funnel has an unnecessary step

Every additional screen between the ad and the "aha" moment — the first successful transaction, the first loan offer shown, the first savings goal set — is a place CAC quietly climbs. Map your actual funnel and count the steps. Kenyan mobile users are patient with M-Pesa prompts because that pattern is familiar; they are not patient with generic multi-step onboarding forms borrowed from a global fintech template.

3. Retargeting budget is under-used

A large share of fintech sign-ups don't convert on the first visit — someone needs to think it over, check with a friend, or simply gets interrupted. Kenyan fintechs that split budget too heavily toward cold prospecting and not enough toward retargeting people who started sign-up but didn't finish are leaving some of the cheapest conversions in their account on the table. Retargeting a warm, already-interested audience is almost always cheaper per conversion than prospecting cold.

A simple audit you can run this week

  • Pull your funnel drop-off by step for the last 30 days — find the single biggest leak first.
  • Check what percentage of budget is going to retargeting versus cold prospecting.
  • Read your top 3 ads as if you were a first-time Kenyan fintech user with no prior trust in the brand — does anything in the first three seconds address "is this legitimate?"

Fixing the funnel and the trust signal usually moves CAC more than any bid strategy change does.
