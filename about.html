Origin

From a Nairobi course to a growth lab.

NGL started as a single growth marketing course cohort in Nairobi, run alongside Khaled's work at Ndovu. It paused for a year — then came back as something more useful: a full-service agency built by people who've actually run growth and agency operations, not just taught them.

→ Course cohort launched, Nairobi
→ 8 participants, strong feedback
→ Paused: local ops dependency
→ Relaunched as full-service agency
How we're built

Lean by design, not by default.

No local hire until revenue justifies it. Remote delivery as the default. Local freelancers per project for physical production. It's a deliberate structure, not a shortcut.
