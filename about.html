About

I'm Khaled. I run the ads myself.

NGL — Nairobi Growth Lab — is a one-operator performance marketing practice. That's a deliberate choice, not a starting point I'll grow out of.

NGL started as a broader idea for a Nairobi-based marketing collective. What's clear a year in is that the highest-value thing I can offer a fintech or e-commerce brand is direct, hands-on performance marketing — not a layered agency structure standing between us.

So that's what NGL is now: paid media, funnels, and content strategy, run by one person who's also the one you'd call if a campaign needs a decision at 9pm.

Background

I lead performance marketing at a fast-growing tech company, running paid acquisition across Meta, TikTok, and Google for a consumer product operating at scale. NGL is where I apply the same discipline — clear targets, structured testing, honest reporting — to smaller, growth-stage brands that don't yet have a full in-house team.

How I work

  • Every account starts with an audit — tracking, past spend, and funnel — before any new budget moves.
  • I bring in trusted local freelancers for production work (video, design, photography) when a project needs it, but strategy and media buying stay with me.
  • Reporting is weekly, specific, and in plain language — what changed, why, and what's next.

Who I work best with

Nairobi-based fintech and e-commerce brands that already have some traction and are ready to put a real system behind paid growth — not a first-time test of whether ads work at all.
