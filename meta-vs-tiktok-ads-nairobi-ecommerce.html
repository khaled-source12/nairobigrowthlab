Meta Ads vs TikTok Ads for Nairobi E-commerce Brands: Which Should You Start With?

Every e-commerce founder in Nairobi asks some version of the same question in the first month of running ads: Meta or TikTok? The honest answer is that both platforms can work here — but they reward different things, and if your first budget is small, the platform you pick first matters more than people admit.

Start with what you're selling, not what's trending

TikTok in Kenya rewards products that are easy to understand in three seconds of video: visually simple, one clear benefit, impulse-friendly price point. Fashion, beauty, phone accessories, and food brands tend to do well because the format does the selling for you.

Meta — specifically Instagram and Facebook feed and Reels placements — still carries more weight for products that need a bit more trust before someone buys: higher price points, anything health-related, or anything where a customer wants to see reviews and a proper product page before checking out.

Where your audience actually is

Kenya's TikTok audience skews younger and more urban than Facebook's. If your product is genuinely aimed at 18–28 year olds in Nairobi, Mombasa, or Kisumu, TikTok's cost per engaged view is usually lower right now simply because competition for that attention is thinner than on Meta.

If your customer is a bit older, or you're selling into a B2B or family-purchase decision, Meta's targeting and retargeting tools are more mature, and WhatsApp integration for Kenyan buyers closing the sale through chat is a real advantage Meta has that TikTok doesn't match yet.

The budget-size test

With a genuinely small first budget, run on one platform, not both. Splitting a small budget across Meta and TikTok usually means neither platform's algorithm gets enough data to optimize properly, and you end up with two mediocre campaigns instead of one that's learning.

  • Choose TikTok first if your product is visual, impulse-buy friendly, and your audience is under 30.
  • Choose Meta first if you need retargeting, WhatsApp checkout, or your audience skews 30+.
  • Add the second platform once the first one is converting profitably and you have creative that's proven to work — much of it can be reformatted rather than rebuilt from scratch.

What doesn't change between platforms

Regardless of which you start on, the same three things determine whether the campaign works: a pixel that's actually firing correctly, a landing page built around one offer, and creative that's made for the platform rather than repurposed from a photoshoot. Get those right first — the platform choice matters less than people think once the fundamentals are in place.
